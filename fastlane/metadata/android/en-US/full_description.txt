Wétéo shows the weather for your location or any city, with no ads, no tracking and no account.

<ul>
<li>Current weather, hourly and 7-day forecasts</li>
<li>Details for each day: temperatures, rain, sunrise and sunset</li>
<li>Rain radar map around the displayed city</li>
<li>City search and a list of favorites</li>
<li>Your choice of units for temperature, wind and precipitation</li>
<li>Light, dark or automatic theme</li>
<li>Available in English, French, German, Italian and Spanish</li>
</ul>

Data comes from <a href="https://open-meteo.com">Open-Meteo</a>, a free and open service. The rain radar and its one-hour forecast come from <a href="https://github.com/JoshuaKimsey/LibreWXR">LibreWXR</a> (CC BY 4.0), with satellite or model estimates outside radar coverage, on an <a href="https://openfreemap.org">OpenFreeMap</a> map (© OpenStreetMap contributors). Your location is only used to request the local weather and is never stored.
